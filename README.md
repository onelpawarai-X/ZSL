## ZSL-X v1.0.0

**Full legal text:** [ZSL-X-LICENSE.txt](ZSL-X-LICENSE.txt)

### Key Points

1. **Commercial Freedom** — Use for any purpose: personal, commercial, enterprise, government, education, research. No royalties, no fees.

2. **Modification Rights** — Study, modify, refactor, rewrite, fork, combine with other works. Create derivative works without restriction.

3. **Redistribution** — Share in source or binary form with or without modifications. Must include license copy and attribution.

4. **Attribution** — Credit onelpawarai as original creator. Reference the project repository. State modifications if any.

5. **Identity Protection** — Don't use "ZYRAXON" branding to imply your derivative is the official release. Descriptive references like "Based on ZYRAXON AI" are allowed.

6. **Contributions** — Contributors grant royalty-free license for their submissions. Ownership retained. Contributions may be included or excluded at Licensor's discretion.

7. **Patent Grant** — Contributors grant patent license for their contributions. Patent litigation terminates all licenses.

8. **Security** — Report vulnerabilities privately first. Reasonable disclosure timeline.

9. **Warranty** — Provided "AS IS". No warranties of any kind.

10. **Liability** — Licensor not liable for any damages.

11. **Auto-Transition** — Automatically transitions to MEKRA-X v1.0.0 on January 1, 2028. No action required.

## FAQ

### Q: Can I use ZSL-X for my commercial product?
**A:** Yes. ZSL-X grants full commercial rights. No royalties, no fees, no permission needed.

### Q: Do I need to open-source my derivative?
**A:** No. ZSL-X is not copyleft. You can keep your modifications private.

### Q: Can I use "ZYRAXON" in my product name?
**A:** You can use descriptive references like "Based on ZYRAXON AI" but cannot use "ZYRAXON" branding in a way that implies your product is the official release.

### Q: What happens on January 1, 2028?
**A:** All software under ZSL-X automatically transitions to MEKRA-X v1.0.0. No action needed. Software obtained before 2028 can stay under ZSL-X if you prefer.

### Q: Can I stay on ZSL-X after 2028?
**A:** Yes, for copies obtained before the transition date. New distributions should use MEKRA-X.

### Q: What if I breach the license?
**A:** Under MEKRA-X, you get a 30-day cure period to fix the breach. Under ZSL-X, termination is automatic.

### Q: Is there a patent risk?
**A:** ZSL-X includes a patent grant from contributors. If you sue for patent infringement, your license terminates (patent retaliation clause).

### Q: Can I contribute code?
**A:** Yes. Contributors retain ownership but grant a royalty-free license for their submissions. The Licensor may attribute contributors in release notes.

### Q: How do I report a security vulnerability?
**A:** Report privately to the Licensor. You'll get a 72-hour acknowledgment and 14-day remediation timeline. You can disclose publicly after 90 days or after a fix, whichever comes first.

### Q: Can I use this license for my own project?
**A:** ZSL-X is designed for the ZYRAXON ecosystem. You're welcome to adapt it, but it's not a general-purpose license like MIT or Apache.

### Q: What's the difference between ZSL-X and MEKRA-X?
**A:** MEKRA-X is the upgraded successor with clearer terms, enterprise rights, contributor protections, and security framework. It auto-activates in 2028.
