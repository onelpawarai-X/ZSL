## The short version

You can **use** ZYRAXON. Freely, personally, at work, at scale. No
permission, no fee, no approval. That is not a loophole — that is
the design.

You can also **read the source, change it, add features, and build
your own projects** with it. Contributions and pull requests are
welcome and encouraged.

Three things are off the table:

1. **You may not sell it.** Reselling the software, a build, a
   template, or a bundled copy is prohibited. The right to sell
   ZYRAXON belongs to its author.
2. **You may not publish a modified version as your own.** Forking
   and contributing back is fine. Relaunching the result as your
   own product is not.
3. **You may not remove its systems.** The subscription and
   entitlement engine, tool and agent-mode gating, the permission
   and security systems, the licensing and activation system, and
   the origin and attribution system must keep working.

Everything else stays open.

## Why the three restrictions exist

This is not gatekeeping. It is arithmetic.

ZYRAXON's paid tiers are sold on the strength of its systems — the
tool registry, the tier gating, the agent modes, the permission and
security enforcement, and the activation model. If someone were
permitted to take the whole thing, remove exactly those systems, and
sell the result as a free competitor, the paid product would fund
its own destruction. That is the specific outcome the restrictions
prevent.

The response to that concern is not "lock everything down." It is
the opposite: the source is open, the Systems are modifiable, you
may build commercial products on top, and the license only closes
the three doors that would otherwise break the economics for
everyone building on it.

## Can I build a paid product with it?

Yes, if the product is yours and the software is a dependency.

You may build an application, distribute it to your users, and
charge for it. What you may not do is distribute the software
itself, or a substantially complete copy of it, or a copy with the
Systems disabled.

In short: **build around it freely; do not resell it, and do not
ship a stripped copy of it.**

If you only use ZYRAXON, you owe the author nothing and need no
approval at any point.

The following must continue to function in any redistribution or
Derivative Work:

- Subscription and entitlement enforcement
- Tool registry and tier gating
- Agent mode gating
- Permission, security, and safety enforcement
- Licensing and activation verification
- Origin, copyright, and authorship notices

You may **reimplement, extend, improve, or add features** to any of
these. You may not remove them, stub them out, or change them so
that they exist only to be bypassed.

## Attribution

If you distribute a Derivative Work built around the software, you
must keep the origin visible: a notice that the software was
originally created by onelpawarai, a reference to the original
repository, a note that modifications were made, and a copy of the
license.

Source comments alone are not enough for a distributed product — the
notice must also appear in your user-facing documentation or product
surface.
